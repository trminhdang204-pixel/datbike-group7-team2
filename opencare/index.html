OpenCare: repair that fits your day

Concept prototype for a university innovation project with Dat Bike. Not an official Dat Bike product.

Store names and addresses come from Dat Bike’s public store list; map positions are approximate. Queues, technicians and parts are simulated. Distances and ready times are calculated live from your clock.

Switch roles at the top of the phone: Customer, Dealer or Dat Bike HQ.
